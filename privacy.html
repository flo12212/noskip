Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 16, 2026

NoSkip Clipper ("the app") is a personal desktop tool. It runs on the owner's own computer, not on a public server.

What data the app uses

  • Basic TikTok profile information of the authorized account (open ID, display name, avatar) to show which account is connected.
  • Access tokens from TikTok, stored only locally on the owner's computer, to upload videos.
  • Public statistics of the account's own videos (views, likes, comments, shares) to see which clips perform well.

What the app does not do

  • It does not collect data from other TikTok users.
  • It does not sell or share data with third parties.
  • It does not use cookies or tracking on this website.

Deletion

Access can be revoked at any time in TikTok under "Security" → "Manage app permissions". Revoking access makes the app delete the stored tokens on next use. For questions, open an issue at the contact link below.
